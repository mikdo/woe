# Drawings

Drop a drawing here as `<slot>.webp` and deploy. It replaces the placeholder (emoji, avatars or the big title) straight away.
The full list, with where each one shows and a good size, is in `lib/art.ts`, and on the Grown-ups page under Admin → Drawing slots (which also shows which are done).

| File | Where it shows | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `home.webp` | Home: the big sky picture at the top | 1200 × 1000, transparent |
| `new-adventure.webp` | Home: corner of the "Start a new adventure" card | 600 × 600, transparent |
| `sign-in.webp` | Sign-in and invite screens: the sky picture | 1200 × 800, transparent |
| `grownups.webp` | Grown-ups: corner of the sky header | 600 × 600, transparent |
| `making.webp` | Ask: while an episode is being made | 800 × 600, transparent |
| `lost-voice.webp` | When the voice credits run out (Cheko lost his voice) | 600 × 600, transparent |
| `library-empty.webp` | Library: when there's nothing to show | 600 × 600, transparent |
| `otto.webp` | Otto's round avatar, everywhere | 400 × 400, square, face centred |
| `cheko.webp` | Cheko's round avatar (otter), everywhere | 400 × 400, square, face centred |
| `cheko-<form>.webp` | Cheko after he transforms: `dino`, `robot`, `dragon`, `doctor`, `diver` | 400 × 400, square |

`style-ref.jpg` is the style reference the cover art is generated from. It isn't a slot.
